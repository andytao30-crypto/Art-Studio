About This Organization


About Andy's Art Studio

  • Founded and operated by 12-year-old Andy with the support of his parents.
  • Provides balloon twisting, face painting, and magic shows for birthday parties, school events, community celebrations, team-building events, and special occasions.
  • Andy is passionate about basketball, art, and making sports and recreation accessible to everyone, including those facing challenges.
  • 100% of proceeds are donated to charity organizations supporting children and athletes in need.
  • Beneficiaries include:
    • Special Olympics Northern California
    • Okizu
    • Programs supporting children and families affected by cancer
  • Event services are offered at $100 per hour.

Past Events

  • Special Olympics Northern California Polar Plunge [you can find the website here and photos of the event here]
  • Altos Academy Lunar New Year Celebration
  • Cub Scouts community events
  • Team-building events
  • Birthday parties and private celebrations
  • Art fairs and community festivals

Our Mission

  • Bring creativity, fun, and smiles to every event.
  • Use art and entertainment to give back to the community.
  • Support children, athletes, and families through charitable fundraising and service.
  • Share the joy of sports, art, and community with everyone.

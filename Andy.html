About Me

Hi! My name is Andy, and I am a 12-year-old student who loves art, basketball, and bringing joy to everybody through creativity and entertainment.

One of my biggest passions is basketball. I love the teamwork, friendships, and life lessons that sports can teach. I believe everyone should have the opportunity to experience the joy of sports and recreation, including children and individuals facing challenges.

I enjoy art, balloon twisting, face painting, and performing simple magic tricks. I love seeing people's reactions when they receive a balloon creation, get their face painted, or experience a fun magic moment.

My goal is to continue learning, serving others, and using my talents to bring happiness, creativity, and encouragement to everybody.
